Project Overview

This blog is part of my work for ENGL 170: First-Year Writing at Lindenwood University. Throughout the Spring 2026 semester, I will be exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, creative writing, and the "attention economy".

The goal of this site is to engage in a networked conversation with other students, analyzing how AI tools impact our intellectual labor and the way we share information online.
